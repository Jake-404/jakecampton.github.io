II.
Case Study · 2023 – present

VeBetter: from zero to 5M+ users.

5.33Musers onboarded
168MThe HiVe launch impressions
52M+reward transactions
50+apps live
$Millionspaid in B3TR rewards
Spotlight Stage, London Blockchain Conference. Speaking with Bernhard Kronfellner, Partner at Boston Consulting Group, on the VeBetter go-to-market.

The challenge was to take blockchain beyond crypto-native audiences and give ordinary people a reason to use it.

VeBetter was built to do exactly that. VeChain's technology abstracts away much of the visible blockchain experience, letting people use apps without needing to understand the infrastructure underneath. We applied that to sustainability, rewarding everyday actions from recycling to greener travel, and took the platform from zero to 5.33M users.

I owned communications across the go-to-market as VeChain moved from enterprise technology into a consumer market: positioning, launch, partnerships, distribution and audience growth. We launched at The HiVe in Las Vegas with Boston Consulting Group as a partner and two Nobel laureates, Sir Konstantin Novoselov and Sir Andre Geim, on stage.

The result proved something bigger: blockchain could disappear into the product experience and still deliver utility at mainstream scale.

I helped shape the go-to-market thesis and narrative with Boston Consulting Group, drawing on market research, partner interviews, investor meetings and conversations with builders, creators and users. I then led its communications delivery across positioning, briefing documents and content for each audience. The work culminated in the VeBetter whitepaper, the founding case for blockchain as sustainability infrastructure, and helped generate 20 app-development engagements, 15 enterprise activations and three hackathons.

From 18,000 token holders on day one to 224,000 twelve months later. 1,144% growth, driven by consistency and clear messaging

The HiVe was the centrepiece. With agency partners I ran the communications arc from pre-event build and executive preparation to real-time social, press and post-event amplification. It landed 18 media placements, with syndication to Bloomberg.

Around the launch, I built the supporting system: blog series, announcements, community briefings, onboarding flows and spokesperson messaging, so every audience and channel launched from the same proposition.

Tonnes of carbon avoided, thousands of litres of water saved, hundreds of kilos of plastic kept out of circulation. Tokenization turned real-world action into verified, rewardable impact on-chain.

I managed paid media and activation spend within VeChain's £500k+ annual UFC sponsorship package, alongside campaigns across X, Instagram, YouTube, Meta and sponsor-owned channels.

The result: 5.33M users onboarded, millions of dollars in B3TR rewards paid for sustainability actions and 52M+ reward transactions recorded on chain. More than 50 apps are live, each tied to a UN Sustainable Development Goal.

This is what I build towards: turning a technical proposition into a product people use, a message people understand and a growth engine that proves the technology works in the real world. VeBetter showed that tokenization could move beyond speculation to measure, incentivise and reward real-world behaviour at consumer scale.

Career

From community and content to communications, growth and AI.

From there, the remit grew into technical communications, PR, product storytelling, go-to-market, paid acquisition and eventually global communications leadership.

I have helped take products from launch to millions of users, built social audiences beyond 1.5M, driven hundreds of millions of impressions and views through paid and organic campaigns, and turned attention into active participation across consumer ecosystems.
